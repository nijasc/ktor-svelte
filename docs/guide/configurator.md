# Configurator

Tick what your app needs, and copy the two blocks into your project. Only settings that differ from the defaults appear, so the result stays short, and the panel on the right recommends what most apps should turn on. Click **Apply** to take a recommendation.

<ConfigBuilder />

Every option is explained in [Gradle settings and tasks](../reference/gradle.md) and [Ktor settings](../reference/ktor.md). For an existing project, `./gradlew svelteConfig` shows what is set now and scores it the same way.
